How I built it

Loaded the notes with pandas, removed punctuation, lowercased, replaced line breaks, tokenized with NLTK, removed English stopwords and numbers, then counted words with pandas and a CountVectorizer document-term matrix (327 notes × 13,869 terms). The SDOH and disease word lists follow the CDC's leading causes of death and common SDOH domains. "Depression" was also searched in the notebook but its counts were not saved in the output, so it is left out here.

Data source

MIMIC-III NOTEEVENTS subset (discharge summaries) provided for MSHI509E Clinical Data Mining and Analytics, Morehouse School of Medicine. MIMIC data requires credentialed access through PhysioNet, so only aggregate counts appear on this page.
